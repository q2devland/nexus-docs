Delivery log

The Delivery log records every call a Webhook has made, each of its tries, and every run of an Action that’s logged, with what the receiver or the Action handler answered. It’s where you find out whether a receiver got what Nexus sent, and why not.

Delivery log, in Settings, is for admins. It has two tabs, Webhooks and Actions; the users who run an Action see its runs in the Item view instead, under Action runs (see Running Actions).

The calls of Webhooks

Section titled “The calls of Webhooks”

The Webhooks tab lists the calls, the latest queued first, under how many there are:

  • Queued: when the change that called it was made.
  • Webhook, Event, Collection and Item: which Webhook called, after which change, of which Item, by its id.
  • Status: Pending while it’s waiting to be made or retried, Succeeded once the receiver answered a 2xx, Failed once it has failed for good.
  • Tries, Last try, with its HTTP status, and Next try, while pending.

Sort it by pressing a header, with Shift, Ctrl or Cmd for a second one. Filters, in the header, keeps the calls of some Webhooks, statuses or Collections, or queued between two days; a Webhook’s See its deliveries opens it filtered to its own. Export to CSV downloads the calls kept, as sorted, with those columns, and Refresh shows those made since.

Reelhouse’s listings service answered every call about its films at once, but two:

  • Brass Orchard, film 4: it was down for maintenance at the first call, and answered 503. Nexus tried again 30 seconds later, and it answered 200: the call Succeeded, in 2 tries.
  • Velvet Static, film 7: it answered 500 every time. After the first try and Film published’s 3 retries, the call Failed for good.

A call

Section titled “A call”

Pressing a call opens it in a panel: its status, Webhook, event, Collection and Item, when it was queued and, while it’s pending, when it’s next tried. Then its Tries, in the order made, each with:

  • what the receiver answered, HTTP and its status, or No answer, with why: it took longer than NEXUS_WEBHOOK_TIMEOUT, or the receiver couldn’t be reached;
  • when it was made and how long it took;
  • the method and URL called, as the Webhook was then;
  • the start of the receiver’s answer, its first 1,000 characters.

And last, the Payload it sent, as every try sent it: see The payload.

When a call fails

Section titled “When a call fails”

A call that failed for good isn’t made again. Read its tries for why: the receiver’s status and answer, a timeout, or a URL that can’t be reached. Once the receiver is fixed, it reads through the API what it missed, by the Collections and Items of the failed calls, which Filters keeps alone and Export to CSV lists.

Retry more, or for longer, by giving the Webhook more Retries, up to 10, or the deployment a longer NEXUS_WEBHOOK_RETRY_DELAY; a receiver that’s often down for a while needs both. A call still pending is made at the URL the Webhook has by then, so correcting a wrong URL saves the calls still to be retried.

A call fails without a try left, too, saying why above its tries, when its Webhook is deactivated or deleted while it waits, or when its Webhook’s signing secret can’t be read, as after the deployment’s NEXUS_ENCRYPTION_KEY changed.

The runs of Actions

Section titled “The runs of Actions”

The Actions tab lists the runs of the Actions that are logged, the latest first: when it was Run on, the Action, its Collection, who it was Run by, on how many Items, its Status, Succeeded or Failed, and, of an Action that calls a URL, the receiver’s HTTP status, under Answer. It’s sorted, filtered by Action, Status, Collection and the days it was run, and exported as the calls are.

Pressing a run opens it: who ran it, when, on which Items, with what values, the message shown to them, what the receiver answered, the URL called or the Action handler run, how long it took, and the payload it was given. A run isn’t retried: whoever ran it saw how it went, and runs it again if they want to.

An Action set not to be Logged leaves no run here, nor in the Item view.

Once a Webhook or an Action is gone

Section titled “Once a Webhook or an Action is gone”

The Delivery log keeps the calls and runs of a Webhook or an Action deleted, under the name it had, marked deleted. Nexus keeps every entry: none is ever removed.

Through the API

Section titled “Through the API”

For the Admin role:

  • /api/deliveries answers a page of the calls; /api/deliveries/csv, all of them as a CSV file.
  • /api/action-runs answers a page of the runs; /api/action-runs/csv, all of them.

Each takes the filters as webhook (or action), by its id, status and collection, by its key, as many times as there are values; from and to, as yyyy-MM-dd, in timezone, UTC unless given; sort, a column, with - before it for descending order, or several between commas; and a page’s offset and limit, 50 unless given, 100 at most.
